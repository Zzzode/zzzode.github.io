## Root causes

1. **Discovery gaps:** broad exact-date searches and source chasing did not adequately investigate Chinese financing, industrial/embodied AI and policy reporting. These are actual gaps even when several generic queries mention the corresponding topic. No story count certifies coverage.
2. **Relevant-result loss:** manual source/candidate selection dropped the Anthropic leads. Filtering shared query-result URLs down to the selected source set also removed the FT and CLS URLs from the public ledger. The original raw responses prove that those results existed. The final ledger was internally consistent but incomplete as an account of relevant results.
3. **An erroneous source gate:** `daily-ledger.ts` required fetched primary publication evidence for every published item. Skill prose emphasized following primary evidence, and the implementation turned that preference into an exclusive requirement. Reliable attributed reporting, interviews and disclosures of plans can be news before an official announcement exists. Shared-source reprints must still not be called independent verification.
4. **Event/time conflation:** the Fengqing assessment asked for the original operational launch date even though the current news was a conference announcement/report. Reporting and underlying activity require separate time models. Conversely, new translations of old interviews cannot refresh old events.
5. **Insufficient review reconciliation:** the independent review checked recorded candidates and performed supplementary discovery, but did not reconcile all relevant raw-result leads with candidate dispositions or properly revisit preceding-issue pending leads. Its original coverage assessment did not justify treating the omitted reporting as harmless limits. The original pass must not be presented as proof of coverage.

## Bounded workflow correction

The separately pushed rule change adds an explicitly attributed reporting evidence path while retaining fetched-body, publication, time-window, maturity, source-chain and independent-review requirements. It requires result inventories before pruning, preservation of changed assessments, prior-issue follow-up and separation of retrospective corrections from current new-event counts. Regression tests cover reliable reporting, rejected snippets/community repetition/missing citations or dates, time buckets and lost prior references. All 22 unit tests passed; Astro check reported zero diagnostics; production build, isolated daily integration and both existing ledger validations passed. An independent reviewer read the five-file rule diff and independently ran the tests before that commit.

Article corrections and their exact sources, resulting item order, hashes, remaining limits and browser review are recorded in each issue's ledger and review report. This audit does not certify that every global AI development has been discovered.

The correction also reopens 40 October 18 evidence-limited exclusions that used missing-primary language; their old assessments remain in `priorAssessments`. Reopening a lead does not establish a new current-window event. Newly read original material resolves several other leads: Meshy's issuer announcement belongs in October 17; Hy-Memory, Dory, FAIRINO, XHEART and the NVIDIA/AI4Bharat/IIT Madras Indic training recipe are retained as original-issue date-boundary observations.
