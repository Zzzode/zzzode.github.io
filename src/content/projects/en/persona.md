description: |-
  A self-hosted SuperAgent hub and multi-platform client system that brings coding agents across your computers and your personal context into one place. Powered by a single Go binary (personad), an always-on Hub connects to Node processes on each of your machines so interactive sessions of Claude Code, Codex, opencode, pi, DSH, and Antigravity CLI stay on your own hardware. You can watch live progress, answer questions, and approve plans from iPhone, Android, Web, Mac, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, Dynamic Island, or Siri, then pick up the exact same session back at your desk.

  Beyond cross-device handoff, Persona builds a shared knowledge base from chats, people, and projects, triages Lark, mail, GitHub, Telegram, and WeChat into an AI-sorted Inbox, and exposes phone data (contacts, calendar, reminders, photos, health) as MCP tools with on-device write confirmation. An extension SDK and public marketplace (Persona Extensions) let you plug in new tools, agent runtimes, data sources, channels, and app views while keeping all data self-hosted.
